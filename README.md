**Ember & Oak** — a wood-fired kitchen cooking game: run a one-chef kitchen through the dinner rush. Take orders from the ticket rail, chop, grill, boil and fry ingredients at their stations, assemble dishes on the pass and serve them before patience runs out. Six original recipes, cooking timers that can burn, combo tips for on-time serves, and a three-day shift that gets busier every day. Pure static files, no network: canvas art drawn in code, sounds synthesized with WebAudio.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — click a station to walk over and use it (or move with WASD / arrow keys and press Space), serve finished plates at the pass.
